Review

Exploding Head

Exploding Head is memorable because the shock happens fast. The camera is close when the head effect hits, so you see it before you can look away. That one quick moment is why the clip sticks with you.

Scene change

What I would change

I would add a short pause before the head explodes. Right now it happens so fast that you barely have time to react. A second of stillness first would make the same effect feel stronger.
